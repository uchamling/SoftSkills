
## Coursework: Individual Report – Academic Summary and Curriculum Vitae (CV)

### Topic Selection Instructions

Before selecting your topic, you must carefully review the **Course Information Form (CIF) – BSc (Hons) Software Engineering Autumn 2026**.

After reviewing the CIF, examine the available **UCIF (Unit/Course Information Form) documents** and select an individual topic from the remaining areas of study that have not already been chosen or allocated.

When choosing your topic, please ensure that:

- The topic is relevant to the Software Engineering discipline.
- The topic is selected from the available UCIF documents.
- The topic is of personal academic and professional interest to you.
- Sufficient academic literature and research materials are available to support your work.
- The selected topic is appropriate for critical analysis within the Academic Summary.

### Submission Requirements

Your coursework submission must consist of:

- **Part A:** Academic Summary (approximately 750 words)
- **Part B:** Curriculum Vitae (CV)

Both parts must be combined and submitted as **one Word document**.

### Lecturer Guidance

Students are encouraged to select a topic that aligns with their interests, career aspirations, and academic strengths. A well-chosen topic will enable you to produce a stronger academic summary and demonstrate a deeper understanding of the subject area.

If you are uncertain about the suitability of your chosen topic, please consult your lecturer before commencing your research and writing.
